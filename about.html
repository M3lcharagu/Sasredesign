About Sasfino

Sasfino is a KEPHIS-accredited wood products company that grow trees, value-add them to make highly sustainable wood products. Our flagship product is wooden pallets for logistic industry.

Our Story

A vital link between the forest and the freight container

Sasfino Farm Limited is a premier agroforestry and manufacturing enterprise dedicated to supporting the agricultural sector's global reach. By integrating sustainable silviculture with advanced industrial production, we manage thriving timberlands that provide the raw materials for our high-quality wood products.

We specialize in the production of durable, export-grade pallets, serving as a vital link in the international supply chain, ensuring that agricultural exports are transported safely and efficiently. At Sasfino, we believe that the journey of a product begins with the health of our forests, hence helping local agriculture grow on a global scale.

Our Mission

"We promote growing the trees that carry your harvest to the world."

Our mission is to empower agriculture with sustainable logistics solutions. We connect local farms to global markets through innovative agroforestry and high-quality wood products, ensuring the integrity of shipments from factory floor to final port.

Every pallet that leaves our factory carries a little of that philosophy with it: the idea that good logistics starts long before a container is sealed, with a well-managed forest, a properly treated board, and a team that takes quality control seriously.
Milestones

Twelve years of growing, one ring at a time

A short history of how a single idea became a working part of Kenya's export infrastructure.

2013

Sasfino Farm Limited is incorporated

Registered in Nairobi under the Companies Act (Cap. 486) as a limited company.

2013 Onward

A closed-loop forestry model takes root

We began managing our timberlands under a simple rule: production should never outpace nature's ability to regenerate. That rule still governs every harvest today.

Ongoing

From timber to treated, export-ready pallets

Our Wood Products Division grows from a single product line into a full range: standard pallets, air freight pallets, sea freight pallet tops, and angle board corners, all heat-treated to ISPM 15 standard.

2025

ISPM 15 accreditation

KEPHIS accredits our heat treatment process, clearing our wood packaging material for international phytosanitary compliance for a further year.

Today

A trusted name at Mombasa and JKIA

Our pallets now move through the Port of Mombasa and Jomo Kenyatta International Airport on behalf of horticultural exporters, tea and coffee estates, and freight forwarders across East Africa.
