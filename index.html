What we do

One roof, from standing timber to shipped freight

Sustainable silviculture and industrial production sit under the same management here, so the timber that supplies our pallets and the pallets that carry your cargo are never more than one handshake apart.

01 / Compliance

Accreditation

Every export-grade unit spends 30 minutes at a core temperature of at least 56°C, clearing international phytosanitary standards so your cargo isn't the thing holding up a port inspection.

02 / Regeneration

Active Reforestation

Every harvested tree is followed by several new seedlings, a standing rule that keeps our forests sequestering carbon and holding the soil in place year after year.

03 / Engineering

Load Capacity

Stringers and deck boards are reinforced into every heavy-duty build, so a pallet rated for 1,200kg still holds its shape after a long haul and a rough stack.

04 / Service

Professionalism

We measure the job by more than the invoice. Good work here also means fair wages, safer forests, and a factory floor the people of Kiambu are proud to work on.

Our forests, our legacy

We only harvest what we've already replaced

Since 2013, a closed-loop forestry model has kept one simple rule at the center of everything we cut: production never outpaces what the land can regrow.

Responsible Sourcing

Every board starts on land managed for biodiversity and long-term ecosystem health, not stripped for a single harvest.

Active Reforestation

Several seedlings go in the ground for every tree we take, keeping carbon locked in and soil where it belongs.

Circular Economy

A damaged pallet gets repaired before it gets replaced, and whatever wood is left over becomes mulch or biofuel, not landfill.
